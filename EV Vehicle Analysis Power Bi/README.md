🧩 Problem:
A new EV maker want to enter in Indian market and wants to know what, where and How much trend of current EV market in India.
🛠 Solution:
I built a Power BI dashboard that analyzes:
✔ Sales trends
✔ Regional and State performance
✔ Category mix”
🧠 Skills Used:
Power BI | DAX | Data Modelling | Power Query
📈 Insight:
Over three years, EV sales grew from ~2.7 lakh to ~10 lakh units, with penetration rising from 2% to 5%, delivering a CAGR of 93.9%. All time Avg. daily units sold is ~2K
.
Segment Mix:
•	Two-wheelers dominate (>90% of sales)
•	Four-wheeler adoption remains slow, likely due to:
o	Limited policy incentives
o	Inadequate charging infrastructure
o	Consumer preference for ICE vehicles
Regional Trends:
•	West + South zones contribute ~70% of cumulative sales
•	Top 5 states:
o	Higher penetration (~6% vs ~4% overall)
o	Contribute ~60% of total sales
% EV Penetration = EVs Sold units/(EV+ICE) sold units
Remark Regarding Data Set sourced from CodeBasics 
We currently have separate state-wise and maker-wise datasets. Combining them into a single table with both Maker and State columns would allow analysis of maker sales trends by state.
Adding fields like City/District, Gender, Price, and Buyer Age would further support more comprehensive analysis.
